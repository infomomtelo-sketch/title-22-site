Using this in a class or a client onboarding

The video is short on purpose — it works as an opener, not a substitute for the module you teach. A few things that tend to help:

  • Play 0:00–0:45 cold at the start of a session, then teach the regulation. Play the rest after, and the software reads as a consequence of the rule rather than a product pitch.
  • For mixed-language staff, turn on Spanish or Tagalog captions before you press play — switching mid-scene loses people.
  • The transcript prints cleanly on its own; the player and navigation drop out of the printed page.
  • Chapter 1:15 stands alone if you only have time for the MAR.

A hard line we don't cross, and worth saying out loud in class: nowhere in this walkthrough does the AI suggest, adjust, or comment on a dosage. It flags missing documentation — the clinical call always stays with the physician and pharmacist.
