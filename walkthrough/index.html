A guided walkthrough of Title22

Six stages, start to finish — from an empty account to a report you can hand an analyst. Scroll it in about a minute.

1
Setup

Tell it about your home

Facility name, licence number, type and licensed capacity. That loads the Title 22 requirements that apply to your home — an RCFE with six beds is not asked for what a 50-bed home is asked for.

2
Compliance checklist

Every requirement as a yes or no

The whole list in one place, answered the way an analyst asks it. Nothing here needs a resident record — you are attesting on a date, and the date is kept.

Fire clearance current, drill log up to date
Every staff member's TB clearance on file
× Emergency disaster plan written and trained
3
Staff & training

It tells you whose card expires

Add each employee once. CPR, First Aid, TB, LiveScan, mandated reporter, the 40-hour initial and the hours logged since — each with its own date, each warning you before it lapses. Employment records, not health records.

CPR — J. Carter20 days
TB — D. ReyesOverdue
40-hour — M. Patel16 of 40
4
Readiness score

One number, and what moves it

A live score across administrator, staff and facility requirements, with the shortfalls listed in the order worth fixing. Tick something off and watch it climb.

71%
Fix these next:
1 · Emergency disaster plan missing
2 · J. Carter's CPR expires in 20 days
3 · D. Reyes' TB clearance overdue
5
Ask Tello

A plain-English answer, built on your own records

Ask what is outstanding and get an answer assembled from what is actually in your account — not a generic FAQ. She will not quote a regulation back at you as settled fact; anything regulatory goes to your licensing analyst.

Am I ready for an inspection?

You're at 94%. Three things stand between you and a clean visit: the emergency disaster plan isn't on file, J. Carter's CPR expires in 20 days, and D. Reyes' TB clearance is overdue.

6
Audit log & export

Something to hand over

Every change recorded, timestamped and attributed — who did it, and when. Export the packet as a PDF when the analyst is at the door.

No PHI storedNo BAA to signNothing to breach

No residents, no medications, no MAR — Title22 holds none of it, by design.
