Step labels

Playback speed

This tour has no spoken narration — just a music bed and the step labels on screen, which are in English. Switching to Español or Tagalog adds a translated label under each step, so the video works with the sound off or in a noisy room.

Using this in a class or a client onboarding

Two minutes and twenty seconds end to end, but it's built to be broken up. A few things that tend to help:

  • Steps 1–3 are setup. Play them once at the start of an onboarding and you'll answer most of the first-week questions before they're asked.
  • Step 4 is the one to pair with your regulation module — it's where the CCR codes appear on screen.
  • Step 7 (Digital MAR) stands alone if you only have time for one segment with direct-care staff.
  • For mixed-language staff, switch the step labels to Spanish or Tagalog before you press play — changing mid-step loses people.
  • The step sheet prints on its own; the player and navigation drop out of the printed page.

A hard line we don't cross, and worth saying out loud in class: nowhere in this walkthrough does the AI suggest, adjust, or comment on a dosage. It flags missing documentation — the clinical call always stays with the physician and pharmacist.
