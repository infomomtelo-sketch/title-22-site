Start Your Home

Twelve steps between deciding to open a California care home and holding a licence. Written plainly, because nobody hands you this list.

This is the usual order of things, in plain words. It is not legal advice, and it is not a statement of what the Department requires. Dates, fees and what an inspector will accept change, and they are your licensing analyst's to give you. Use this to keep track of where you are.

Stage 1

Get Certified

Kumuha ng SertipikoObtenga su certificaciónਸਰਟੀਫਿਕੇਟ ਲਵੋ

The part that is about you. None of it needs a house.

  1. 1

    Take the administrator certification course

    Kunin ang kurso para sa administratorTome el curso de certificación para administradoresਐਡਮਿਨਿਸਟ੍ਰੇਟਰ ਸਰਟੀਫਿਕੇਸ਼ਨ ਕੋਰਸ ਕਰੋ

    The certification course for RCFE administrators, run by a vendor the Department has approved. The vendor issues the certificate when you finish. Photograph the certificate when it arrives.

  2. 2

    Pass the state administrator exam

    Ipasa ang pagsusulit ng estadoApruebe el examen estatal de administradorਸਟੇਟ ਐਡਮਿਨਿਸਟ੍ਰੇਟਰ ਇਮਤਿਹਾਨ ਪਾਸ ਕਰੋ

    The exam is taken through the state, separately from the course. You are sent the result. Photograph it.

  3. 3

    Get your Live Scan fingerprint clearance

    Magpa-Live Scan at kunin ang clearanceObtenga su autorización de huellas Live Scanਲਾਈਵ ਸਕੈਨ ਫਿੰਗਰਪ੍ਰਿੰਟ ਕਲੀਅਰੈਂਸ ਲਵੋ

    You are fingerprinted at a Live Scan location, and the clearance comes back from the Department’s background check bureau. Photograph the clearance, not the receipt.

  4. 4

    Get a CPR and First Aid card

    Kunin ang CPR at First Aid cardObtenga su tarjeta de CPR y primeros auxiliosCPR ਅਤੇ ਫਸਟ ਏਡ ਕਾਰਡ ਲਵੋ

    The cards a training provider issues when you complete the class — usually both in one session. Photograph the cards.

Stage 2

Prep House

Ihanda ang BahayPrepare la casaਘਰ ਤਿਆਰ ਕਰੋ

The building itself. Photographs, not descriptions.

  1. 5

    Choose the house

    Piliin ang bahayElija la casaਘਰ ਚੁਣੋ

    Yours or leased. Photograph the front of the house from the street, the way somebody arriving would see it.

  2. 6

    Get the fire clearance

    Kunin ang fire clearanceObtenga la autorización de bomberosਫਾਇਰ ਕਲੀਅਰੈਂਸ ਲਵੋ

    The clearance is issued by the fire authority for the area the house is in — they inspect and then issue it. Photograph the clearance they give you.

  3. 7

    Bedrooms, bathrooms and the way out

    Mga kwarto, banyo at labasanDormitorios, baños y las salidasਸੌਣ ਵਾਲੇ ਕਮਰੇ, ਬਾਥਰੂਮ ਅਤੇ ਬਾਹਰ ਜਾਣ ਦਾ ਰਸਤਾ

    Photograph each bedroom, each bathroom, and every door someone would leave by. One picture each is enough.

  4. 8

    Kitchen, food storage and hot water

    Kusina, imbakan ng pagkain at mainit na tubigCocina, almacenamiento de alimentos y agua calienteਰਸੋਈ, ਖਾਣੇ ਦਾ ਭੰਡਾਰ ਅਤੇ ਗਰਮ ਪਾਣੀ

    Photograph the kitchen, where food is stored, and the water heater with its setting visible.

Stage 3

Get License

Kumuha ng LisensyaObtenga la licenciaਲਾਇਸੰਸ ਲਵੋ

The application itself, and the visit at the end of it.

  1. 9

    Go to the licensing orientation

    Pumunta sa orientation ng lisensyaAsista a la orientación de licenciasਲਾਇਸੰਸਿੰਗ ਓਰੀਐਂਟੇਸ਼ਨ ਵਿੱਚ ਜਾਓ

    The Department runs an orientation for new applicants and gives you the application packet there. Photograph anything they hand you.

  2. 10

    Fill in the application packet

    Punan ang application packetComplete el paquete de solicitudਅਰਜ਼ੀ ਦਾ ਪੈਕੇਟ ਭਰੋ

    The forms in the packet from orientation, filled in and signed. Photograph the completed packet before you send it, so you have your own copy.

  3. 11

    Pay the application fee

    Bayaran ang bayad sa aplikasyonPague la cuota de solicitudਅਰਜ਼ੀ ਦੀ ਫੀਸ ਭਰੋ

    The fee is paid to the Department as part of the application. Photograph the receipt or the confirmation.

  4. 12

    The licensing visit

    Ang pagbisita ng licensing analystLa visita del analista de licenciasਲਾਇਸੰਸਿੰਗ ਵਿਜ਼ਿਟ

    An analyst comes to the house before a licence is issued. Photograph the paperwork they leave with you.
