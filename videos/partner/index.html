Where these numbers come from

Every figure in this video is read out of the product, not out of a pitch. The 90-day trial is the default on a trainer code and the app resolves a referred signup's trial length from it. The 20% is the default on the same row, stored against your code and read at payout — your own rate is agreed individually, so confirm it before you share anything.

The two warnings are the ones worth eighty seconds of anyone's time. Only signups are recorded, never clicks — there is no click tracking in the product at all. And a code cannot attach to an account that already exists: if the person has logged into Title22 before, your link will not claim them and there is no way to fix it afterwards. Send it before they sign up.

Commission is paid directly, by a person, not by the software — there is no automated payout inside the product. The written version of all of this is on the partner page, and the partner tour is a recording of the real screen rather than a set of cards.
