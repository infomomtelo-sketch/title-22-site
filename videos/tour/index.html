What you are looking at

Every frame is the live Title22 interface. The facility is invented — a six-bed RCFE called Marigold House with five made-up staff — and the tour is the app's own guided tour, the same one built into the product, driven end to end.

You will not see a resident anywhere in it, and that is not an omission for the camera. Title22 Lite holds no resident records, no medications and no medication administration record, so there is nothing of the kind to film. What it does hold is on screen: the readiness score, staff certifications with dates against them, the Title 22 checklist, documents, roles, and an assistant that answers from those records and nothing else. More on that on Features and Security.
