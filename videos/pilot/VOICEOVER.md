# The 30-Day Proof — voiceover script

For `videos/title22-30-day-proof.mp4` (44.7 s). Each line has a window; say
it inside that window. The times are the caption changes in the video, so the
voice and the picture move together.

About 2.5 words a second is a calm pace. Every line below fits its window at
that pace with a breath to spare.

| # | Starts | Window | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0 s | 3.6 s | Which of your homes do you look at first? |
| 2 | 3.6 s | 5.6 s | Title22 runs beside what you already use — and it holds no resident health information. |
| 3 | 9.2 s | 5.1 s | In the Tello tab, the portfolio briefing puts every home on one screen. |
| 4 | 14.3 s | 6.3 s | One row per home, lowest first: checklist, overdue items, expired certifications, open clearances. |
| 5 | 20.6 s | 4.3 s | Every number is counted by the app — not guessed by the AI. |
| 6 | 24.9 s | 6.1 s | The second row is an illustration: the same home, with every open item closed. |
| 7 | 31.0 s | 4.7 s | Then a short summary — for someone who has a minute. |
| 8 | 35.7 s | 5.2 s | Two of your homes. Thirty days. Press it on day one, and on day thirty — and compare. |
| 9 | 40.9 s | 3.8 s | The 30-Day Proof. No card. Nothing to cancel. |

Line 6 says "illustration" on purpose — that row is not a real facility, and
the video must say so.

## Recording it

- Phone voice memo is fine. Quiet room with soft things in it (a closet full
  of clothes is the classic); no fan, no TV.
- Phone about a hand-span from your mouth, a little to the side so the "p"s
  don't pop.
- **Record each line as its own clip** (easiest to place exactly), or one
  take with a two-second pause between lines. Either works.
- Say each line two or three times if you like — the best take gets used.
- Warm and plain, like telling a colleague. Not an announcer.

Send m4a, mp3 or wav. Clips get placed at their start times above, levelled,
and mixed under a quiet music bed if one is supplied; the captions stay in the
picture for people watching muted.
