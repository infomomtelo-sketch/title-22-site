What you are looking at

Every frame is the live Title22 interface on a phone, and it is the app's own guided tour — the same one built into the product — driven end to end. The partner screen comes first because that is the part a trainer or an affiliate is asking about: one row per code, how many people signed up through it, how many of those are paying, and the rate on record.

The second step is the one worth pausing on. A code does not only attribute a signup, it sets that person's trial length — the number on the row is the one they get, instead of the default thirty days.

You will not see a resident anywhere in it, and that is not an omission for the camera. Title22 Lite holds no resident records, no medications and no medication administration record, so there is nothing of the kind to film — the menu in the recording has no Residents, MAR, Medications or Daily-log entry for the same reason. The facility here is a new, empty one, which is why the readiness score reads 7 of 119. More on that on Features and Security.

The commission figure on screen is the field's default, not a quoted rate. Rates are agreed per partner — see Affiliates for how that works, and For Trainers for the classroom side.
