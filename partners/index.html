What does a code do?

Their trial is longer, usually 90 days instead of 30. Their account is linked to you for good. You see signups and paying counts for your code.

What do I earn?

A share of every paid month, for as long as they stay. Trainers get 20%; other rates are agreed with Eli. Plans are $29 or $79 a month. Eli pays you directly. Pricing →

Two things to know first

Signups count, not clicks. And a code only attaches to a brand-new account, so send your link before they sign up.

Who to send it to

New administrators, homes that are hiring or opening a second home, and consultants. Not mass lists or strangers. Always say you earn a commission.

Teaching too?

A classroom account is a separate request. Sign up for a free account first, then ask for both.
