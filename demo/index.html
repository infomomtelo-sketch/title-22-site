Title22 AI

Software that knows your facility, not just the regulation.

AI Compliance Assistant

Ask: "Is Room 4's medication log complete for July?" Get an answer grounded in the actual Title 22 section, with the citation attached.

Readiness Score

Upload a new CPR certification. The facility's readiness score moves from 91 to 94, live.

Daily Briefing

Every morning: 2 certifications expire this week, 1 incident follow-up is overdue, 3 checklist items are due.

AI Document Review

Upload your medication policy. Get back: "Missing PRN documentation procedure — required under the applicable Title 22 section."

Incident Report Drafting

Enter what happened in a few fields. Get a structured, compliant incident narrative back — edit it, then submit.

Deficiency Triage

A citation comes in. Title22 ranks it by severity and drafts a corrective action plan before your next call with the analyst.

Append-Only Audit Log

Every entry: who, what, when. Nothing edited, nothing deleted — exportable as a single file for a surveyor.

A hard line we don't cross: nowhere in Title22 does the AI suggest, adjust, or comment on a dosage. It flags missing documentation — the clinical call always stays with the physician and pharmacist.
