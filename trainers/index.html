Questions trainers ask

Do I have to change my course?

No. You teach your own course, with your own lessons and quizzes. Title22 doesn't supply course content and isn't a certification. It's the hands-on practice space and the record that follows your students into their work.

How is the 20% paid?

Each signup through your link is tracked to you. When a home you referred pays, you earn 20% of that plan for every month it stays subscribed, and Title22 pays you directly. Program details

Do I need to tell students I earn a commission?

Yes. Say it plainly when you share your link. It keeps your recommendation honest and your students' trust intact.

What does Title22 cost the homes my students work for?

After the free trial, $29 a month for one home, or $79 a month for up to five. See pricing.

Does Title22 store resident health records?

No. Title22 holds staff files, training and clearance dates, and facility documents. It holds no resident records, medications or MAR. How we handle data

I teach CE only. Can I still join?

Yes. Whether you teach initial certification, continuing education, or both, email us and we'll set you up.
