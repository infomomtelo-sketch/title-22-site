Is any resident information involved?

No. No resident records, no medications, no MAR. Incidents are written without naming anyone.

Can the AI make up numbers?

No. The app counts every figure first. Tello only summarises them. A home that can't be read is flagged, never shown as on track.

What does it cost?

Nothing for 30 days. After that, Multi-Home is $79 a month for up to five homes. No plan? The account turns read-only and nothing is deleted. Pricing →

Does it guarantee an inspection result?

No. It shows open items and helps close them. Regulatory questions go to your licensing analyst.
