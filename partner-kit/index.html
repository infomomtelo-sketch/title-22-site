Before you share
  • Say you earn a commission. Every image carries a small "partner link" line, and every post below says it. Keep it in. The FTC requires it.
  • Only use the trial length on your code. Affiliates: until Eli registers your code, people get the standard 30 days.
  • Send to people you know, one at a time. Don't text or email lists of strangers.
  • Don't change the claims. Title22 doesn't hold resident records or medications, and it doesn't guarantee an inspection result. If you're asked something you're not sure about, point people to hello@title-22.com.
