No resident health information, by design.

Title22 Lite holds no resident records, medications or medication administration records. There is nothing to breach, nothing to subpoena from us, and no business associate agreement needed — because the data is not here. Staff records (TB clearance, LiveScan, certifications) are employment records, not medical ones.

Every facility's records are walled off from every other.

Access is enforced at the database itself, keyed to your facility license — not just hidden in the interface. Your staff files, documents and incident reports are only ever visible to your own account and the people you invite. Another operator on Title22 cannot see your facility, and you cannot see theirs.

Encrypted in transit and at rest.

Every connection to Title22 runs over HTTPS/TLS, and your data is stored on managed, established cloud infrastructure that encrypts it at rest. Nothing about your facility travels or sits in plain text.

Role-based access, and no shared logins.

Each person gets their own login and role. Caregivers see only what their shift requires; administrative records stay protected. Every person has their own login, so every entry is permanently attributed to the individual who made it.

A permanent record of who did what, and when.

Changes to staff, documents, checklist items and incidents are written to an append-only, timestamped audit trail. It's the clean, complete history a DSS licensing analyst expects to see — and it means nothing has to be reconstructed after the fact.

Your records are yours. Export or leave anytime.

Title22 is built with no lock-in. You can export your facility's records whenever you want, delete them whenever you want, and cancel whenever you want — and if you ever leave, your data leaves with you. We keep your business by being useful, not by holding your records hostage.

We never sell your data.

Your facility's information is used to run Title22 for you — nothing else. It is never sold, rented, or handed to advertisers. Staff clearances and certifications are employment records and are treated as confidential.

How the AI assistant uses your data — and the line it won't cross.

When you ask the AI assistant a question or open the daily briefing, the relevant facility data needed to answer is sent to our third-party AI provider under commercial terms that do not permit using your data to train their models. And the hard line stays hard: the AI never suggests, corrects, or comments on clinical dosage. Medication decisions belong to physicians and pharmacists — the AI is limited to documentation completeness and compliance guidance.

Title22 helps you see and close gaps. Staying licensed is your facility's job. Questions? hello@title-22.com
