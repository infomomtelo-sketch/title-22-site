Quick answers

Full FAQ →
How much does it cost?

Your first care home is free. $29/month for one home, $79/month for up to five. Every trial is 30 days, with no card and nothing to cancel. Students of a partner trainer get 90 days.

Does this count toward my staff’s 40-hour / 20-hour annual?

These are staff courses from CTC, a CDSS-approved provider, and the hours count toward the annual requirement. The New Hire RCFE Pack carries 4 hours of listed courses plus Orientation. Administrator renewal CE is separate.

My staff works at 2 homes. Do they take the course twice?

No. They take it once. The completion files to their staff record and shows across every home they work in.

Is there a MAR or resident records?

No. Title22 holds no resident health information: no resident records, no medications, no MAR. That’s why there’s no business associate agreement to sign. Keep using what you use today for medication administration.

Who built it?

Built from real facility operations, with requirements guided by a certified California RCFE administrator.

Does the AI give medical advice?

Never. Tello never suggests, corrects or comments on medications or dosage, on any plan.
