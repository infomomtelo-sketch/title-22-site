Questions operators ask first

The four we hear most. Read the full FAQ →

Does Title22 replace our paper binders completely?

Day to day, yes — Title22 moves the records you work from every shift into one digital record, every entry timestamped and permanently attributed to the caregiver who logged it, with no shared logins. It does not take over your licensing obligations: Title22 supplements, and does not replace, your facility's record-keeping duties, so keep your own copies of the records your license requires you to retain.

Which CDSS licensing forms are mapped inside the software?

The core modules map to the documents an analyst checks during a visit: LIC 622 — the centrally stored medication record under §87465 — as a digital MAR with gap flagging and PRN reason and follow-up tracking; LIC 602A (physician's report / resident appraisal); and staff files with certification tracking.

Does the AI make clinical decisions or adjust medication doses?

No — this is a hard line. Title22's AI never suggests, corrects, or comments on clinical dosage information. Medication decisions belong to physicians and pharmacists. The AI is limited to documentation completeness and compliance guidelines.

We operate multiple facilities. Can we track them all together?

Yes. Administrators switch between facility locations from a single dashboard account, while every resident record, staff log, and license stays separated per facility license number.
