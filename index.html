How much does it cost?

$29/month for one home, $79/month for up to five. Every trial is 30 days, with no card and nothing to cancel.

Is there a MAR or resident records?

No. Title22 holds no resident health information: no resident records, no medications, no MAR. That’s why there’s no business associate agreement to sign. Keep using what you use today for medication administration.

Who built it?

Built from real facility operations, with requirements guided by a certified California RCFE administrator.

Does the AI give medical advice?

Never. Tello never suggests, corrects or comments on medications or dosage, on any plan.
