# Share assets

`title22-share-card-1200x630.png` is the one canonical card. It is the
`og:image` and `twitter:image` on all 49 public pages, so it is what
appears whenever anyone shares title-22.com in a message, a post, or a
search preview. If it changes, it changes everywhere at once — there is
no per-page variant and there should not be one.

## What a share asset may claim

Anything on a share card is a public claim about a regulatory product,
made where nobody can read the fine print. The rules in CLAUDE.md apply
in full, and two of them bite hardest here:

- No absolute compliance claims. We do not guarantee compliance or any
  inspection outcome.
- The audit log is "append-only" — never "immutable" or
  "tamper-evident".

Three more that come from how the sites read today:

- The score is a **readiness score**, not a compliance score. Readiness
  is what the product measures; compliance is what the licensee remains
  responsible for, and the Terms say so.
- Requirements are scoped: Title 22 Division 6 Chapter 8, LIC 622,
  LIC 602A and the MAR. Not "every requirement".
- No personal attribution. Both sites say "requirements guided by a
  certified California RCFE administrator" and nothing about who builds
  it. Settled Sep 2026.

## Dates inside a screenshot

If a card shows the app, every date visible in it has to be in the
future relative to publication. A compliance product whose own
screenshot shows a year-old "upcoming" expiry is demonstrating the
failure it sells against. Re-shoot rather than re-date by hand, so the
rest of the screen stays consistent with it.

## Rejected: the wide "Compliance made simple" banner (Sep 2026)

Not added, and not referenced anywhere. It was shared for review and
carried three problems:

- Its "Upcoming Expirations" panel showed Aug 2025 dates — over a year
  expired at the time it was reviewed — and two conflicting dates per
  row.
- "Multilingual … English, Spanish, Tagalog, Punjabi" and "Voice-Ready"
  describe capabilities that do not exist. The app has no language
  support and no speech input of any kind. The only multilingual assets
  are the demo video's English, Spanish and Tagalog captions, which are
  machine-translated and awaiting a native speaker's review (see
  videos/README.md). There is no Punjabi anywhere.
- "By someone who's been there" reintroduces the personal attribution
  both sites had just dropped.

Its dashboard mock also read "85% compliance" and showed a sidebar
(Reports, Tasks) that the app does not have, while omitting MAR,
Incidents and Ask Tello.
