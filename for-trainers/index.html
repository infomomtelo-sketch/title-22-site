The trainer walkthrough — what to show your class

Use this flow when demonstrating Title22 to your students. The guided walkthrough at title‑22.com/walkthrough/ follows this same order — open it alongside your classroom account.

1. Intro

Open with the compliance problem

Start by naming what every administrator fears: a DSS visit when documentation isn't ready. Title22 is the system that closes those gaps before the analyst arrives — not after.

2. Dashboard

Show the trainer dashboard overview

Log in and walk through the main dashboard: the facility readiness summary, flagged documentation gaps, and expiring staff certifications. This is the first thing an administrator sees every morning.

3. Training

Demonstrate a class or training module

Show how to create a training class from CDSS source material, assign it to learners by role, and set completion requirements. This is where the "CDSS vendor readiness" story comes to life — policy turns into structured, trackable training.

4. Records

Walk through medication and compliance records

Open the digital MAR and show how entries are timestamped and attributed. Show the LIC 622 and 602A record sections. Point to exactly what a DSS licensing analyst checks — and how Title22 has it ready.

5. Roles

Explain role-based access

Show the difference between administrator, supervisor, caregiver, and read-only access. Stress why this matters for an audit: every record is attributed to a specific person with a specific role — no shared logins, no ambiguity.

6. Progress

Show completion tracking and proof

Walk through the training completion view: who finished, who hasn't, who needs a follow-up. This is the audit-readiness story — evidence that staff were trained, on record, before the inspection.

7. CTA

Close with the free trial

End with a direct CTA: your students can start a 14-day free trial at title22.app using your trainer code. The first inspection they pass with it in place is proof your training worked.

Common questions from trainers

Do I need to change my curriculum to use Title22?

No. Title22 is a supplementary tool, not a replacement for your course content. You keep teaching the same regulations; Title22 gives your students a live system to practice on and take home.

How does the classroom account work?

You get a full Title22 account pre-loaded with a demo facility — residents, staff with an expiring certification, medications, an incident, and a working MAR. The account never expires and you can use it in every class you run.

How does the 20% commission track?

Your students sign up through your personal referral code. When they convert to a paid subscription, 20% of their monthly plan is credited to you — automatically, every month they stay subscribed. See commission details →

Is this only for ICTP trainers, or can CE-only trainers participate?

Both. Any DSS-approved CE or ICTP trainer whose students become RCFE administrators can partner. If you're unsure whether your program qualifies, reach out and Eli will confirm.
