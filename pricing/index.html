Both plans start with a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. Tello works during the trial too. Plans are started and managed inside the Title22 app at title22.app — same product, the secure app just lives on its own domain.

What neither plan holds: no resident records, no medications, no MAR, no LIC 601 or LIC 602A. That is a design decision, not a gap — keeping resident health information out is what lets Title22 run without a business associate agreement, and it is why the price is $29. Facilities keep using whatever they use today for medication administration.

A hard line we don't cross: Title22's AI never suggests, corrects, or comments on clinical dosage information.
