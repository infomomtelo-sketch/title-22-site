About the real exam

How many questions are on the RCFE administrator exam, and how long do I get?

The state exam given by the CDSS Administrator Certification Bureau is 100 multiple-choice questions with a 120-minute time limit. This practice exam mirrors that: 100 questions, 120 minutes on the clock.

What score do I need to pass?

70% or more — at least 70 of the 100 questions. The same mark is used to score your practice sitting here.

Is the real exam open book?

Yes. On the state exam you may use printed copies of Title 22 and the Health & Safety Code (electronic copies and personal notes are not permitted). Knowing how to find a rule fast is a real exam skill — practice it here.

Are these the actual state exam questions?

No. The state's questions are not published. Every question here is original, written from the same public regulations — Title 22, Division 6, Chapter 8 and Health & Safety Code sections 1569 and following — with the citation shown for every answer so you can look it up yourself.

How long do I have to pass the state exam after my training course?

After completing the 80-hour Initial Certification Training Program, you have 60 days and up to three attempts to pass, and the exam fee is $100. If you pass, you must apply for your administrator certificate within 30 days.
