Practice on the real system. Tello walks you through it.

The classroom opens straight into a sample care home — no signup, no card, nothing to install. Tello, the Title22 assistant, guides you button by button while you practise. Nothing you do is saved.

What you’ll practice with

A pre-loaded facility designed for learning. Everything is real except the people — every staff member in it is invented, and the practice roster is fixed, so no real person’s information can be entered.

Your classroom facility includes:

  • 5 staff members with certification gaps (one CPR expiring in 20 days, one TB test overdue, one expired First Aid)
  • A caregiver working before their LiveScan cleared, with no mandated reporter training on file
  • Two people short of the 40-hour initial training — 16 hours and 8 hours in
  • Two incidents waiting for documentation
  • A dashboard that opens “amber” — you have work to do from day one

Nothing you do here touches a real record, and nobody can see your practice — there are no accounts, no invites, and no scores. Your practice lives only in your browser tab and clears when you close it.

How it works

1

Open the room

One tap opens the sample home — no signup, no card. Tello’s panel opens on its own at the side of the screen.

2

Follow Tello, button by button

She walks you through adding a staff member, scanning the sample CPR card, checking the dates, and saving — one button at a time. Tap the wrong thing and she tells you.

3

Finish on the morning briefing

The practice ends on the briefing owners read every day. The closing screen shows your trainer’s code and the 90-day free trial it carries.

Teaching a class?

Your students practise free in this room. Your own classroom link — carrying your code and the 90-day student trial — is on your partner kit page. When a student later subscribes, you earn 20% for as long as they stay.

Questions?

Is this the same as the real Title22?

Almost. You see the same screens, work with the same tools, and learn the same workflows. The difference: the classroom uses a sample home with invented people, and nothing you practise touches a real facility’s records.

Can my trainer see what I’m doing?

No. There is no account and nothing is saved, so there is nothing for a trainer to see — no attempts and no scores. Your trainer’s link simply carries their code, so if you later start your own Title22 account through it, you get their 90-day trial.

What happens when I graduate?

When you get a job at a facility, you’ll subscribe to Title22 to use the real system there — and you’ll already know how it works, because you practised in the classroom.

Is there a cost?

The classroom itself is free, with no signup and no card. If you start your own Title22 account through your trainer’s link, you get a 90-day free trial, no card needed. After that it’s $29 a month for one home — only if you choose to subscribe. Nothing is charged unless you choose it.
