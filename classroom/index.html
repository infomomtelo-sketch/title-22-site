What you'll practice with

A pre-loaded facility designed for learning. Everything is real except the people.

Your practice facility includes:

  • 8 residents with different care levels
  • 5 staff members with certification gaps (one CPR certificate expiring in 20 days, one TB test 10 days overdue)
  • 10 active medications across those residents
  • 30 days of medication administration history
  • Two open incident reports

Nothing you do here touches a real record. Every fix you make is practice. If you want your trainer to look at your work, invite them to your own facility as a read-only team member from Team access — they see it, they cannot change it. Tello, the built-in AI assistant, is there to answer your questions as you go: she is a study aid, not your trainer, she can be wrong, and she will not give medical advice about a resident.

Questions?

Is this the same as the real Title22?

Almost. You see all the same screens, work with the same tools, and learn the same workflows. The only difference: your practice facility is pre-loaded with teaching scenarios instead of real resident data.

Can my trainer see what I'm doing?

Only if you invite them. Your practice facility is yours, and nobody else can see into it. To let a trainer review your work, open Team access in Title22 and send them a Read Only invite to your facility — they can look at what you have entered but cannot change any of it. You can remove the invite whenever you want.

What happens when I graduate?

When you get a job at a facility, you'll subscribe to Title22 to use the real system there. You already know how it works because you practiced in it.

Is there a cost?

Signing up at title22.app starts a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. Extended student access beyond the trial is granted by email, case by case: write to hello@title-22.com and tell us about your training program. When you become an administrator or operator, you subscribe to the service you'll use at your facility.
