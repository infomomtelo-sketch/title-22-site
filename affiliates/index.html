FAQ

Questions about the program

How do I get paid?

Email your referral code, name, and payment details to hello@title-22.com. Eli confirms your setup and handles payouts directly — no platform middleman.

What counts as a paid subscription?

Only facilities that convert from the free trial to a paid monthly plan trigger your commission. Empty signups and free-trial accounts don't count — this keeps referrals meaningful.

Does my referral link expire?

No. Once your code is registered, it tracks every signup that comes through your link indefinitely. There's no time limit on the relationship.

I'm a trainer, not just an affiliate — is there a better path?

Yes. The trainer partnership includes a free classroom account pre-loaded with a demo facility, a personal trainer code, and 20% recurring commission — see that page for details.
