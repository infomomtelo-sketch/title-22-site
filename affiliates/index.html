What people ask — and the straight answers

Most people who hesitate are asking one of these. Each answer is how the product works today.

"Will I get charged?"

No. Title22 never asks for a card to start, so nothing can be charged and there is nothing to cancel. The trial is 30 days.

"Do I have to put in my facility's details?"

No. An email is all signup asks for. They start in a practice home with made-up staff. Even when they add their own home, the license number is optional.

"Does it hold my residents' information?"

No. Title22 holds no resident records, no medications and no MAR. Staff certifications and training are employment records, and incidents are written without naming anyone.

"What if I get busy for a week?"

Nothing is lost. When the 30 days end the account turns read-only — everything they entered stays, and they can pick a plan whenever they are ready.

"Will this get me through an inspection?"

Say it straight: it shows what is open and helps close it, but no software guarantees an inspection result.
